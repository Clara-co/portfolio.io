02 · TEXT MINING

AI vs Human Text Detection

Text mining and classification

Most research hunts for the “AI fingerprint.” We flipped the question: which features best identify human writing? Using about 487,000 labeled texts, I helped build a logistic regression classifier that reads short word fragments (subword n-grams), punctuation and clause markers to tell human text from AI text. Error analysis then showed where it went wrong, so we could fix those mistakes and improve it.

487,000labeled texts
90%accuracy

Why it matters: My classifier separates AI from human writing with 90% accuracy and shows that people explain their reasoning with words like “because,” while AI leans on formal phrasing like “In conclusion.”

05 · MACHINE LEARNING

AI Sentiment Classifier

Movie review sentiment with noisy crowd labels

Test accuracy by labeling method

Majority vote0.67
Reliability weighted vote0.70
Instance weighting by annotator agreement0.80

I trained Naive Bayes classifiers on movie reviews and compared three ways to handle annotator disagreement: majority vote, reliability weighted vote, and weighting each review by annotator agreement. I also tuned feature thresholds to reduce overfitting.

0.67 to 0.80test accuracy gain
t = 4best feature threshold

Why it matters: I found that weighting reviews by how strongly annotators agreed raised accuracy from 67% to 80%, a practical way for companies to get reliable insight from noisy customer feedback.
